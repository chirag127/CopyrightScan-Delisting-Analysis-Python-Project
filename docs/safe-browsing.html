Interactive — No Bulk CSV
Data source: Google Transparency Report — Safe Browsing.

Key Findings

  • Phishing sites now outnumber malware sites roughly 10:1.
  • Detected unsafe sites peaked around 2016–2017; declined as browser warnings became effective.
  • Safe Browsing protects 5 billion+ devices across Chrome, Android, Firefox, and Safari.
  • Social engineering attacks rose sharply from 2014 as malware dropped.
  • Weekly phishing site detections remain above 100,000 in recent years.
