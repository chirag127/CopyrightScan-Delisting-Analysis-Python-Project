Overview / EU Right to be Forgotten

EU Right to be Forgotten (Delisting)

Privacy-based URL delisting requests to Google Search under EU law, since the landmark 2014 CJEU ruling.

Interactive — No Bulk CSV
Data source: Google Transparency Report — EU Privacy.

Key Findings

  • Over 1 million URLs requested for delisting since May 2014.
  • Approximately 46% of evaluated URLs were delisted.
  • France, Germany, and the UK are consistently the top requesting countries.
  • Request volume surged after the 2014 ruling and has plateaued in recent years.
  • Most common reasons: personal information, professional misconduct, criminal records.
