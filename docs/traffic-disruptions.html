Traffic Disruptions

Documented anomalies in Google traffic — typically government-ordered internet shutdowns, DNS blocks, or infrastructure disruptions.

Interactive — No Bulk CSV
Data source: Google Transparency Report — Traffic.

Key Findings

  • Events documented in 60+ countries since 2012.
  • Longest sustained disruptions in North Korea, Turkmenistan, and Cuba.
  • Election-related disruptions increased significantly post-2018.
  • Disruptions cluster around political events: elections, protests, exam periods.
  • Sub-Saharan Africa has seen increasing frequency of politically motivated shutdowns.
