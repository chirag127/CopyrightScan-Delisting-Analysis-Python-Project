Overview / Government Removal Requests

Government Requests to Remove Content

Requests from governments worldwide to remove content from Google services. Published bi-annually per country.

Interactive — No Bulk CSV
Data source: Google Transparency Report — Government Removals. No bulk CSV is published; figures below are from the interactive report.

Key Findings

  • Requests grew from ~1,000 in H1 2011 to 10,000+ in recent periods — roughly a 10× increase.
  • Top requesting countries include Russia, Turkey, India, South Korea, and Brazil.
  • Compliance rate varies widely — some countries see less than 10%, others over 80%.
  • Court orders generally see higher compliance than direct government requests.
  • Defamation is the most cited legal basis globally; hate speech is most common in Germany and France.
