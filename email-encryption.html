Overview / Email Encryption

Email Encryption in Transit

TLS encryption rates for inbound and outbound Gmail traffic. Near-universal encryption achieved by 2020.

Interactive — No Bulk CSV
Data source: Google Transparency Report — Email Encryption.

Key Findings

  • Gmail-to-Gmail traffic has been 100% TLS encrypted since 2015.
  • Inbound encrypted traffic rose from ~55% (2014) to over 95% (2023).
  • Outbound encrypted rose similarly — from ~60% to over 95%.
  • Domains without TLS support are now a small minority of email senders.
  • Top domains with poor encryption tend to be legacy corporate and government mail servers.
