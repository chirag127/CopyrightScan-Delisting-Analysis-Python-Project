Overview / HTTPS Encryption

HTTPS Encryption in Transit

Percentage of traffic encrypted across Google products and top-1000 websites. A story of near-universal adoption.

Interactive — No Bulk CSV
Data source: Google Transparency Report — HTTPS.

Key Findings

  • Google Search and Gmail reached 100% HTTPS before 2016.
  • Top-1000 site HTTPS adoption rose from ~48% (2015) to over 95% (2023).
  • Chrome's HTTPS-first mode and browser security warnings accelerated the final holdouts.
  • Android Chrome traffic: 97%+ encrypted as of 2023.
  • macOS Chrome traffic: 98%+ encrypted, the highest across all platforms.
